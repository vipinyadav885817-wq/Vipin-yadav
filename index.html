GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need prior experience? +

No. The learning paths are designed to start with approachable fundamentals, and you can choose where to begin.

Can I learn at my own pace? +

Yes. Lessons and progress are available from your dashboard on the same browser where you created your account.

Is this a real authentication service? +

This website is a front-end learning demo. Accounts and progress are stored in this browser only. Do not use a real or reused password. A production service needs a secure backend and verified password-reset flow.

Are the security lessons intended for real-world systems? +

Learning should stay ethical and authorized. Only practice on systems you own or have explicit permission to test, preferably in a dedicated training lab.
